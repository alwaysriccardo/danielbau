# Facebook App Review - Submission Guide

## Permission Justifications

### For `pages_read_engagement` Permission:

**Why do you need this permission?**
We need to access engagement data from our Facebook business page to display our portfolio of interior renovation work on our website.

**How do you use this permission?**
We use the Facebook Graph API to fetch posts, photos, and videos from our business page that showcase our interior renovation projects. This content is then displayed in a portfolio gallery section on our website (danielbau.ch) to showcase our work to potential clients.

**What user benefit does this provide?**
This allows website visitors to see our latest interior renovation projects directly on our website, providing a seamless experience without leaving our site. It helps potential clients view our work portfolio in one place.

**Data usage:**
- We only access our own business page's public content
- We display photos and videos in a gallery format
- Content is cached for 1 hour to improve performance
- No user data is collected or stored
- No third-party access to any data

---

### For `pages_read_user_content` Permission:

**Why do you need this permission?**
We need to read posts, photos, and videos from our Facebook business page to display our interior renovation portfolio on our website.

**How do you use this permission?**
We fetch photos and videos that we post on our Facebook page showcasing our interior renovation work (before/after photos, project videos, completed renovations). This content is displayed in a portfolio section on our website to showcase our services and completed projects to potential clients.

**What user benefit does this provide?**
Website visitors can view our latest interior renovation projects and completed work directly on our website, making it easier for potential clients to see our portfolio and services without navigating to Facebook.

**Data usage:**
- We only access content from our own business page
- We display photos and videos in a gallery/lightbox format
- Content is cached for performance (1 hour cache)
- No personal user data is accessed
- No data is shared with third parties
- Only public page content is displayed

## App Description

**App Name:** DANIELBAU Portfolio

**App Description:**
DANIELBAU Portfolio is a website integration that displays photos and videos from our Facebook business page on our company website. We use this to showcase our interior renovation work, completed projects, and services to potential clients visiting our website. The app fetches public content from our Facebook page and displays it in a portfolio gallery section, allowing visitors to view our latest work without leaving our website.

**Use Case:**
Display our Facebook page content (photos and videos of interior renovation projects) on our business website portfolio section.

**User Type:**
Business/Page owner (ourselves) - we only access our own page's content.

## Submission Checklist

Before submitting for App Review:

- [ ] App description is filled out in App Dashboard
- [ ] Permission justifications are written (use text above)
- [ ] Privacy policy page is created on your website
- [ ] Privacy policy URL is added to App Dashboard
- [ ] Screenshots are prepared (4 screenshots minimum)
- [ ] Video walkthrough is created (optional but recommended)
- [ ] All required fields in App Review are completed
- [ ] App is in "Development" mode (can submit from development mode)

---

## Tips for Approval

1. **Be specific:** Mention "interior renovation work" and "portfolio showcase"
2. **Show it's your page:** Emphasize you only access your own business page
3. **Clear use case:** "Display our work portfolio on our website"
4. **No user data:** Emphasize no personal data is collected
5. **Simple privacy policy:** Keep it straightforward and accurate

---

## After Submission

- Facebook typically reviews within 1-7 business days
- They may ask follow-up questions
- You'll receive an email when approved
- Once approved, regenerate your access token with the new permissions

---

## If Rejected

If Facebook rejects your submission:
- Read their feedback carefully
- They'll tell you what's missing
- Update your submission based on their feedback
- Resubmit with improvements

Good luck with your submission! 🚀
